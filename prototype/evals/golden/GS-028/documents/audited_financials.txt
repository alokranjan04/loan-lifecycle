Silverline Precision Works LLC: Audited Financial Statements, key figures
Our auditors issued an unqualified opinion on the accounts.
Year to 31 March 2023: turnover USD 11.64m, operating profit before depreciation and amortisation (EBITDA) USD 2.30m.
Year to 31 March 2024: turnover USD 12.61m, operating profit before depreciation and amortisation (EBITDA) USD 2.47m.
Year to 31 March 2025: turnover USD 13.62m, operating profit before depreciation and amortisation (EBITDA) USD 2.64m.
Finance costs for the latest year were USD 0.16m and profit after tax USD 1.45m.
Borrowings at the year end stood at USD 2.13m; scheduled loan repayments of USD 0.40m fell due in the year.
Working capital: current assets USD 5.45m against current liabilities USD 3.76m.
Members' funds: USD 6.81m.
