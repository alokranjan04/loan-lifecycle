Portal submissions

8-agent pipeline

Each agent enriches a single CaseFile, passed end-to-end
1
IntakeAgent
Validates all required document types are present and readable before processing.
→ document inventory
2
DocIntelligenceAgent
Extracts ~45 fields from 6 doc types with Claude. Every value cites its source document and line.
→ extracted_fields.json
3
CompletenessAgent
Identifies missing or expired items and drafts an RM request. Flags blocking vs. non-blocking gaps.
→ rm_request.txt
4
DueDiligenceAgent
Screens borrower, directors and UBOs against sanctions and PEP databases. Any match triggers EDD.
→ flags + Gate 1
5
PolicyEligibilityAgent
Runs 12 policy checks, each citing the exact credit-policy clause. Computes PASS / FLAG / FAIL.
→ 12 policy_checks
6
RiskAgent
Computes 9 financial ratios, scores 8 factors, assigns risk grade B1–D4 and indicative 1-year PD.
→ grade + scorecard
7
ApprovalWorkflowAgent
Routes on risk grade, exceptions and facility size. Enforces delegated authority and triggers credit gate.
→ authority + Gate 2
8
DisbursementConditionAgent
Generates conditions precedent — legal, financial and operational requirements before funds release.
→ conditions_tracker.json
⚑ Gate 1 Compliance EDD
Triggered by DueDiligenceAgent on PEP/sanctions match. Compliance officer clears before credit proceeds. Lower roles blocked and logged.
⚖ Gate 2 Credit Committee
Triggered by ApprovalWorkflowAgent based on risk grade and exceptions. Board Credit Committee approves under full SoD.
✓ Decision Issued & audited
Decision summary, conditions tracker and hash-chained audit log written. Verify with --verify flag.
