SUNRISE COLD CHAIN LOGISTICS LTD
BOARD RESOLUTION

At a duly convened meeting of the Board of Directors held on 2026-10-08 at the registered office:

RESOLVED THAT the Company be and is hereby authorised to borrow up to USD 3,800,000 from First Commercial Bank under a secured term loan facility for a tenor of 60 months for the purpose of acquisition and fit-out of refrigerated warehouse premises at Bhiwandi Logistics Park.

RESOLVED FURTHER THAT Kavitha Rajan (Managing Director) and Arjun Subramanian (Director, Finance) be jointly authorised to execute all facility documentation, security documents and ancillary agreements on behalf of the Company.

Date of resolution: 2026-10-08
