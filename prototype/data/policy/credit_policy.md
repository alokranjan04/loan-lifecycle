# Commercial Credit Policy (Illustrative, v3.2)

Fictional policy written for the prototype. Clause IDs are cited by the agents in every observation.

## 2. Eligibility

### POL-2.1 Business vintage
The borrower must have at least 3 years of continuous operating history, evidenced by audited financial statements for the last 3 financial years.

### POL-2.2 Leverage
Total Debt / EBITDA after the proposed facility must not exceed 4.0x. Between 3.5x and 4.0x is permitted but must be flagged as elevated leverage.

### POL-2.3 Debt service coverage
Projected DSCR (average over the loan tenor) must be at least 1.25x. The latest historical DSCR must be at least 1.20x. A projected DSCR between 1.25x and 1.40x requires a cash-sweep or DSRA condition.

### POL-2.4 Loan to value
For secured term loans, the facility amount divided by the realisable value of primary collateral must not exceed 70%.

### POL-2.5 Liquidity
Current ratio (current assets / current liabilities) must be at least 1.10x.

### POL-2.6 Promoter contribution
For project finance, promoter equity contribution must be at least 25% of total project cost.

## 3. Due diligence

### POL-3.1 Collateral valuation currency
Collateral valuation reports must be dated no more than 180 days before the credit decision date and prepared by an empanelled valuer.

### POL-3.2 KYC and beneficial ownership
Identity documents for all directors and every beneficial owner holding 25% or more must be valid on the decision date. Expired documents must be refreshed before disbursement.

### POL-3.3 Sanctions, PEP and adverse media
All directors, beneficial owners and the borrower entity must be screened. Any politically exposed person (PEP) match requires Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) and sign-off by Compliance before approval. A confirmed sanctions match is an automatic decline.

### POL-3.4 Mandatory documents
A complete application requires: application form, 3 years audited financial statements, 12 months bank statements, KYC/UBO declaration, collateral valuation report (secured loans), project report (project finance), latest tax returns, and a board resolution authorising the borrowing.

## 4. Portfolio limits

### POL-4.1 Sector appetite
Agri and food processing is a "Grow" sector. Commercial real estate is "Maintain". Coal mining and thermal power are "Exit" sectors and are not eligible for new lending.

### POL-4.2 Single obligor limit
Total exposure to a single obligor group must not exceed USD 25 million.

## 5. Conduct of account

### POL-5.1 Returned items
More than 2 returned (bounced) payments in the last 12 months is an adverse conduct observation and must be explained by the relationship manager.

### POL-5.2 Revenue reconciliation
Annual bank credits should reconcile to at least 80% of reported revenue. A lower ratio indicates possible revenue overstatement or banking with other lenders and must be investigated.

## 6. Delegation of authority

### POL-6.1 Approval authority
Facilities up to USD 2 million: Senior Credit Manager. Above USD 2 million up to USD 10 million: Regional Credit Committee. Above USD 10 million: Board Credit Committee. Any facility with an open policy exception escalates one level.

### POL-6.2 Segregation of duties
The person who prepared or analysed the credit proposal cannot approve it. Overrides of the system recommendation require a written rationale that is retained in the audit record.

## 7. Conditions

### POL-7.1 Conditions precedent
Open documentation or due diligence gaps that do not breach eligibility may be converted into conditions precedent to first disbursement, with an owner and a due date.
