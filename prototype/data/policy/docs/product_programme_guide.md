# Product Programme Guide, Document Checklist and Standard Conditions Library (Illustrative)

Fictional document for prototype. Defines the commercial lending product suite, the document checklist for each product, and the bank's standard conditions precedent and ongoing covenants.

## 1. Product Suite

### PPG-1.1 Term loan
A fixed-amount facility repaid in scheduled instalments over a defined tenor. Maximum tenor: 7 years (standard), 12 years (infrastructure / project finance). Interest: fixed or floating (base rate + spread). Security: first-charge collateral required above USD 1 million.

### PPG-1.2 Revolving credit facility
A committed line that may be drawn, repaid, and redrawn within the availability period. Maximum tenor: 3 years, extendable by 1 year with credit committee approval. Interest: charged on drawn amounts only. Commitment fee: 40 bps per annum on undrawn balance.

### PPG-1.3 Working capital demand loan
A short-term facility (up to 12 months) for working capital needs. Repayable on demand or at maturity. Security: hypothecation of current assets (stock and debtors). Reviewed annually.

### PPG-1.4 Project finance loan
A non-recourse or limited-recourse facility for a defined infrastructure or industrial project. Tenor up to 15 years. Requires project report, independent engineer's report, and promoter equity contribution of at least 25% of total project cost.

### PPG-1.5 Trade finance facilities
Includes documentary letters of credit, bank guarantees, and supply chain finance. Governed by the Trade Finance DOA and product programme, not this guide.

## 2. Mandatory Document Checklist

### PPG-2.1 All loan types
Required for every application: (a) completed application form signed by authorised signatories; (b) 3 years audited financial statements; (c) 12 months bank statements for all accounts; (d) KYC/UBO declaration with supporting identity documents; (e) latest income tax returns (2 years); (f) board resolution authorising the borrowing and naming authorised signatories.

### PPG-2.2 Secured facilities
Additional documents for secured loans: (g) collateral valuation report from empanelled valuer (dated within 180 days); (h) title deeds or asset registry extract; (i) existing charge search (land registry or corporate registry); (j) insurance certificate naming the Bank as loss payee.

### PPG-2.3 Project finance
Additional documents for project finance: (k) detailed project report prepared by the borrower; (l) independent technical / engineer's report; (m) environment impact assessment where applicable; (n) project cash flow model (auditor-reviewed); (o) offtake agreement or demand assessment.

### PPG-2.4 Document completeness check
The Completeness Agent verifies that all mandatory documents are present before the application proceeds to credit analysis. Missing documents generate a finding with severity "high" and the application is returned to the RM for completion.

## 3. Standard Conditions Precedent

### PPG-3.1 Financial conditions
Before first drawdown: (a) signing of facility agreement and all security documents; (b) registration of charges with the relevant registry; (c) delivery of insurance certificates; (d) confirmation that no material adverse change has occurred since credit approval.

### PPG-3.2 Compliance conditions
Before first drawdown: (e) KYC refreshed and approved; (f) EDD cleared if a PEP flag was raised; (g) board resolution delivered and verified; (h) no sanctions hits unresolved.

### PPG-3.3 Project finance conditions
Additional for project finance: (i) achievement of financial close (all co-financing in place); (j) independent engineer's certificate confirming readiness to commence; (k) promoter equity injected to the project account.

## 4. Ongoing Covenants

### PPG-4.1 Financial covenants
Standard covenants for term loans: (a) maintain leverage (Debt/EBITDA) below the policy maximum tested annually; (b) maintain DSCR above the policy minimum tested semi-annually; (c) maintain current ratio above 1.10x tested quarterly; (d) notify the Bank within 5 business days of any material adverse change.

### PPG-4.2 Information covenants
Borrower must deliver: (a) audited annual accounts within 120 days of financial year end; (b) management accounts within 45 days of each quarter end; (c) compliance certificate signed by a director confirming no covenant breach; (d) copy of auditor's management letter within 30 days of receipt.

### PPG-4.3 Negative covenants
Without prior Bank consent: the borrower may not (a) incur additional financial debt above USD 500,000; (b) make any dividend payment if leverage exceeds 3.5x; (c) dispose of any charged asset; (d) change the nature of its principal business; (e) make any acquisition above USD 1 million; (f) change its directors if the change would result in a change of control.

### PPG-4.4 DSRA covenant
Where projected DSCR is between 1.25x and 1.40x at approval, the borrower must fund a Debt Service Reserve Account (DSRA) equal to 3 months of scheduled debt service within 30 days of first drawdown.

## 5. Conditions Subsequent

### PPG-5.1 Post-disbursement monitoring
For facilities above USD 5 million, the Credit Administration team conducts a post-disbursement utilisation check within 60 days to confirm that funds have been applied to the stated purpose.

### PPG-5.2 Annual review
All revolving facilities and working capital demand loans are reviewed annually. The RM prepares a renewal credit memo at least 60 days before the facility maturity date.
