# Delegation of Authority Matrix (Illustrative)

Fictional document for prototype. Defines credit approval tiers, escalation rules, and sign-off requirements.

## 1. Scope and Purpose

### DOA-1.1 Purpose
This matrix governs who may commit the Bank to credit exposure and under what conditions. It applies to all commercial, corporate, and project finance facilities originated through any channel.

### DOA-1.2 Scope
Applies to new facilities, limit increases, tenor extensions, restructures, and waivers of financial covenants. Does not apply to trade finance instruments with tenor below 90 days, which are governed by the Trade Finance DOA.

### DOA-1.3 Conflict resolution
Where two DOA tiers appear equally applicable, the higher authority level prevails. The Chief Credit Officer (CCO) resolves disputes in writing within 3 business days.

## 2. Approval Tiers

### DOA-2.1 Senior Credit Manager
May approve facilities up to USD 2,000,000. Authority is per obligor group (aggregated exposure). May not approve where the Relationship Manager (RM) is the same individual or reports to the same Business Head.

### DOA-2.2 Regional Credit Committee
May approve facilities above USD 2,000,000 up to USD 10,000,000. Quorum is three members including at least one from Credit Risk. Minutes must be signed by the Chair within 24 hours of the meeting.

### DOA-2.3 Board Credit Committee
May approve facilities above USD 10,000,000. Also has exclusive authority over Country Risk exceptions and connected-party transactions above USD 500,000. Meeting minutes circulated within 48 hours of the meeting.

### DOA-2.4 Policy exceptions escalation
Any facility requiring a policy exception (waiver of a financial covenant, sector limit breach, or valuation age override) is escalated one full authority tier above what the facility amount alone would require.

### DOA-2.5 Emergency delegations
In the absence of a quorum-capable committee, the CCO may invoke emergency single-signature authority for facilities up to USD 5,000,000, subject to ratification at the next scheduled committee meeting.

## 3. Segregation of Duties

### DOA-3.1 RM independence
The Relationship Manager who sponsors a proposal may not also approve it at any tier. The RM's name must appear in the application header and is excluded from the approver pool.

### DOA-3.2 Credit analyst independence
The Credit Analyst who prepares the credit memo may not sign off as an approver. Their name is logged in the audit trail and excluded from the valid-approver list.

### DOA-3.3 Override rationale
Where an approver's decision differs from the system recommendation, a written rationale of at least 20 characters must be recorded in the loan origination system and attached to the credit file.

## 4. Compliance Sign-off

### DOA-4.1 Compliance Officer gate
Any facility where screening flags a PEP match or adverse media hit requires a separate EDD Cleared sign-off from a Compliance Officer before the credit approval is actioned. The two sign-offs are recorded independently in the audit log.

### DOA-4.2 MLRO referral
Where the Compliance Officer cannot clear an EDD finding within 5 business days, the case is referred to the MLRO (Money Laundering Reporting Officer) for a decision to proceed or file a Suspicious Activity Report.

## 5. Record-keeping

### DOA-5.1 Audit trail
Every approval action, including the approver's name, role, decision, rationale, and timestamp, is logged in the hash-chained audit log. The log is tamper-evident and retained for 7 years.

### DOA-5.2 Annual review
This matrix is reviewed annually by the CCO and approved by the Board Risk Committee. Version and effective date are recorded in the policy registry.
